About Us

Yongkang Baisuikang Technology Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2005 and based in Yongkang — China's renowned hardware manufacturing hub — we are a technology enterprise integrating R&D, production, and sales of cleaning appliances and healthcare products.

With over a decade of expertise, our in-house R&D team handles everything from industrial design and electronic engineering to mold manufacturing and mass production. We operate a complete international OEM/ODM production system backed by strict modular quality control.

We believe in building strategic partnerships based on mutual benefit, long-term cooperation, and shared growth. Our factory continuously launches innovative products for distributors, direct sales companies, and brand partners worldwide.
